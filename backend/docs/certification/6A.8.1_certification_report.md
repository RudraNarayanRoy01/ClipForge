## 4. SchedulingStatus Certification
- **Certified**: `SchedulingStatus` remains explicitly separated from execution status. It accurately describes the scheduling disposition (`READY`, `QUEUED`, `DEFERRED`, `BLOCKED`, `REJECTED`).

## 5. RuntimeExecutionStatus Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionStatus` remains a distinct enum answering "where is this execution in its lifecycle?" containing exactly `PREPARED`, `READY`, `EXECUTING`, `COMPLETED`, `FAILED`, `ABORTED`.

## 6. RuntimeExecutionOutcome Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionOutcome` remains distinct. It correctly contains exactly `SUCCESS`, `FAILED`, and `CANCELLED`. It correctly does not contain `REJECTED` or `ABORTED`.

## 7. Execution Eligibility Certification
- **Certified**: `SchedulingStatus.READY` is the sole explicitly execution-eligible scheduling status. The coordinator specifically verifies this. Non-READY decisions are explicitly rejected and never reach `RuntimeExecutor`.

## 8. Session Boundary Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionSession` remains a passive, immutable, structural bounded-context dataclass. It has no execution logic, no transitions, and mutability is correctly prevented by dataclass freezing.

## 9. State Boundary Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionState` remains a passive, immutable structural representation of lifecycle position. No transition logic or mutability was added.

## 10. Coordinator Boundary Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionCoordinator` remains precisely an eligibility and handoff boundary. It checks for `READY`, delegates to the executor identically (preserving exact object references via kwargs), and returns the exact result without mutations or reclassifications. 

## 11. Executor Boundary Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutor` remains the owner of the actual execution attempt, handling the timing, exception boundaries, and `RuntimeExecutionResult` construction.

## 12. Result Boundary Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionResult` remains immutable, terminal, and historical. It is only constructed after an actual execution attempt (not from scheduling rejections).

## 13. Identity Continuity Certification
- **Certified**: `RuntimeExecutionIdentity` remains the sole canonical identity mechanism. Handoff tests explicitly verify that exact object identity (`is`) is propagated directly from the `coordinate()` boundary down into `execute()`.

## 14. ABORTED vs CANCELLED Certification
- **Certified**: `ABORTED != CANCELLED` is explicitly enforced and documented. `ABORTED` remains a structural lifecycle reset status that does not map to a `CANCELLED` outcome, which only applies to an actual interrupted attempt.

## 15. COMPLETED vs SUCCESS Certification
- **Certified**: `COMPLETED` is explicitly decoupled from `SUCCESS`. The documentation cleanly separates `COMPLETED` as the conclusion of the lifecycle machinery, whereas `SUCCESS` is the specific terminal outcome of the attempt.

## 16. FAILED Status vs FAILED Outcome Certification
- **Certified**: The string value `FAILED` correctly exists independently in `RuntimeExecutionStatus` and `RuntimeExecutionOutcome` without type conflation, preserving lifecycle vs factual attempt semantics.

## 17. Transition Matrix Certification

It correctly reflects the progression from preparation to terminal states, without regression, and accurately identifies `ABORTED`'s source states as unconstrained structural resets.
