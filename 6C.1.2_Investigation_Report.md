## 6. Pipeline Completeness Audit
- **Present:** Application-level monolithic use cases, `RenderExecutionService`.
- **Missing Stages:** There is no orchestrator connecting `RuntimeExecutionBoundary` to `ExecutionEngine`. In tests, they are manually chained.
- **Dead/Bypassed Stages:** `RuntimeExecutionBoundary`, `ExecutionEngine`, `ExecutionMechanismRegistry`, and the entire `runtime/core/` and `runtime/invocation/` hierarchy are completely orphaned from production execution.
- **Orphaned/Duplicate Stages:** `RenderExecutionPipeline` and `RenderExecutor` are completely uncalled and superseded by `RenderExecutionService`.
- **Test-only Stages:** The manual integration test pipeline (`test_transcription_execution.py`) manually composes `Boundary -> Engine`, which is a test-only pipeline.

## 7. Pipeline Ordering Audit
**Architectural Expectation:**
`ExecutionIntent` → `RuntimeExecutionBoundary` (Normalizer) → `ExecutionAdmission` → `ExecutionEngine` (Registry) → `ExecutionMechanism` → `ExecutionResult`.

**Actual Production Ordering:**
HTTP Request → `GenerateClipsUseCase` → `TranscriptionRequest` → `ITranscriptionService` (Direct bypass).

The architectural ordering is completely ignored in production.

## 8. Pipeline Duplication Audit
- `RenderExecutionPipeline` and `RenderExecutor` are orphaned duplicates of the responsibilities owned by `RenderExecutionService` (Application Layer) and the certified `ExecutionEngine` (Runtime Layer).

## 9. Bypass Audit
- **REAL PRODUCTION BYPASS:** `GenerateClipsUseCase` directly injects and invokes `ITranscriptionService` and `ILLMReasoningEngine`.
- **REAL PRODUCTION BYPASS:** `ClipGenerationPipelineService` delegates to `RenderExecutionService`, completely bypassing the Runtime Execution architecture.

## 10. Production-vs-Test Graph Comparison
- **Production Graph:** Application services orchestrate directly to providers (e.g., `use_cases.py` calls `transcription_service.transcribe()`).
- **Test Graph:** `test_transcription_execution.py` manually instantiates `ExecutionTarget`, injects `RuntimeExecutionBoundary` and `ExecutionEngine`, and manually coordinates the handoff.
- **Conclusion:** The integration tests prove the structural correctness of the architecture but falsely certify its active participation in the application.

## 11. Production DI Relationship
`ExecutionEngine`, `RuntimeExecutionBoundary`, and `ExecutionMechanismRegistry` are correctly registered in `runtime_module.py` as singletons. However, they are NEVER resolved or injected by the presentation or application modules (e.g., `videos.py` router directly injects `GenerateClipsUseCase`).

## 12. Architecture Test Audit
`test_runtime_pipeline_architecture.py` and `test_runtime_pipeline_certification.py` successfully enforce structural decoupling and invariant constraints (e.g., `RuntimePipeline` cannot spawn threads). However, they only prove the components are syntactically pure, not that they are part of the active production execution.
