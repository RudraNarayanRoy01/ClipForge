# Milestone 5.6 Quality Assessment

## 1. Technical Quality
* Modules demonstrate high cohesion and loose coupling.
* Code passes static analysis and defined testing standards.
* Interfaces are well-defined.

## 2. Documentation Quality
* High fidelity and readability.
* Documents are strictly markdown-based and version-controlled.
* Matrices and registers provide at-a-glance status updates.

## 3. Architecture Quality
* Clean architecture boundaries verified.
* Dependency direction strictly enforced.

## 4. Operational Quality
* Operational risk identified and mitigated.
* Health readiness and deployment models meet the certification bar.
