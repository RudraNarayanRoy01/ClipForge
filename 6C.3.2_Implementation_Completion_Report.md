## 5. DI Graph After
- `ILLMReasoningEngine` resolves to `RuntimeReasoningAdapter`.
- `ITranscriptionService` resolves to `RuntimeTranscriptionAdapter`.
- `RuntimeInvocationFacade` resolves successfully.
- `RuntimeProviderRegistry` is instantiated, populated, and registered as a singleton.
- `ExecutionMechanismRegistry` contains both `LLMExecutionMechanism` and `WhisperExecutionMechanism`.

## 6. RuntimeProviderRegistry Composition
The registry is constructed in `RuntimeModule` and populated with structural identifiers mapping to `ProviderDescriptor`s for `whisper` and `ollama`. It is successfully registered into the global container.

## 7. LLM Mechanism Composition
`IAIProvider` is resolved from the container within the `RuntimeModule` to instantiate `LLMExecutionMechanism`. The mechanism, alongside a newly added `LLMNormalizer` fulfilling the `WorkloadNormalizer` protocol, is registered into the core execution registries.

## 8. Whisper Mechanism Composition
The concrete `WhisperTranscriptionService` is explicitly registered in `InfrastructureModule` to separate it from the `ITranscriptionService` domain port. The `RuntimeModule` resolves this concrete service and injects it into `WhisperExecutionMechanism`, effectively mapping the legacy provider into the runtime without breaking composition.

## 9. Application Adapter Convergence
Both reasoning and transcription domain ports (`ILLMReasoningEngine` and `ITranscriptionService`) are now bound strictly to their respective Runtime adapters. The application no longer has direct visibility into mechanisms, hardware, or models.

## 10. Circular Dependency Audit
No circular dependencies were introduced. The Whisper mechanism resolves the concrete provider while the application resolves the adapter, safely terminating the injection chain.
